How Squads Work

1

Create or Join

The captain creates the squad and shares its join code. Join a squad with that code while logged in.

2

Fill the Squad

A squad holds 4 to 5 players. Each player has one active squad.

3

Apply Together

Squads with at least 4 members apply from the tournament page. The admin reviews and assigns squads.

4

Get the Room

Assigned squads get the Room ID and password on their dashboard at match time.

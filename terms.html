Terms and Conditions

1. Using this website

This website is for the PlayZyro PUBG community: tournaments, schedules, upcoming events and winners. By creating an account you agree to use the website for community tournaments only.

2. Accounts

You are responsible for your account details and for keeping your password to yourself.

3. Squads

A squad has 4 to 5 members. The captain creates the squad and shares its join code only with the players who will join it. A player can be in one active squad at a time. A squad needs at least 4 members to apply for a tournament.

4. Tournaments and assignments

Applying for a tournament does not guarantee a place. The admin selects and assigns teams to tournaments, and assigned teams see their assignment in their own panel.

5. Room ID and password

At match time, the room ID and password are shown to the assigned squad on its own page. Squad members must not share room details outside their squad.

6. Not included

This website does not provide payment, deposit or withdraw services, and there is no chat between users on this website.

7. Content

Posters, winners and team photos are added when they are provided. Placeholder slots on the website are not results.

8. Changes

Tournament details, schedules and events can be updated by the admin. These terms can be updated on this page.
